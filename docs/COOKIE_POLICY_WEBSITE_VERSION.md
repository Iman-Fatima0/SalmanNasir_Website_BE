# Cookie Policy

**Last Updated:** [Date]

## What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit our website. They help us provide you with a better experience by remembering your preferences and understanding how you use our site.

## How We Use Cookies

We use cookies for the following purposes:

### 🔐 Essential Cookies
**Required for the website to work**
- Keep you logged in to your account
- Protect your account from unauthorized access
- Ensure website security

**These cannot be disabled** - they are necessary for the website to function.

### ⚙️ Functional Cookies
**Improve your experience**
- Remember your language preference (Arabic/English)
- Remember your course progress
- Store your dashboard settings

**You can disable these**, but some features may not work properly.

### 📊 Analytics Cookies
**Help us improve the website**
- Understand how visitors use our website
- Identify popular courses
- Improve website performance

**You can disable these** - they don't affect website functionality.

### 🔗 Third-Party Cookies
**Used by external services**
- OAuth login (Google, Facebook, LinkedIn, Apple)
- Payment processing (if applicable)

**You can disable these**, but OAuth login may not work.

## Your Choices

### Accept or Reject Cookies

When you first visit our website, you'll see a cookie consent banner. You can:
- ✅ **Accept All** - Allow all cookies
- ❌ **Reject Non-Essential** - Only allow essential cookies
- ⚙️ **Customize** - Choose which cookies to allow

### Change Your Preferences

You can change your cookie preferences at any time:
1. Click the "Cookie Settings" link in the footer
2. Or visit: `/cookie-settings`
3. Or go to: Account Settings → Privacy → Cookie Preferences

### Browser Settings

You can also control cookies through your browser:
- **Chrome:** Settings → Privacy → Cookies
- **Firefox:** Options → Privacy → Cookies
- **Safari:** Preferences → Privacy → Cookies
- **Edge:** Settings → Privacy → Cookies

**Note:** Blocking essential cookies may prevent you from logging in or using certain features.

## Cookies We Use

| Cookie Name | Purpose | Duration | Type |
|------------|---------|----------|------|
| `auth_token` | Keeps you logged in | Session or 30 days | Essential |
