## Introduction

This Cookie Policy explains how Elcandi ("we", "us", or "our") uses cookies and similar tracking technologies on our website (the "Service") and how you can control them.

## What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are placed on your device (computer, tablet, or mobile) when you visit our website. They help us provide you with a better experience by remembering your preferences and understanding how you use our website.

## Types of Cookies We Use

### 1. **Essential Cookies** (Strictly Necessary)

These cookies are necessary for the website to function properly and cannot be switched off. They are usually set in response to actions you take, such as logging in or setting privacy preferences.

**Purpose:**
- Authentication and security
- Maintaining your login session
- Preventing fraudulent activity
- Ensuring website security

**Examples:**
- `auth_token` - Stores your authentication token for logged-in sessions
- `session_id` - Maintains your session state
- `csrf_token` - Protects against cross-site request forgery attacks

**Duration:** Session cookies (deleted when you close your browser) or up to 30 days for "Remember Me" functionality

**Can be disabled?** No - These are essential for the website to work

---

### 2. **Functional Cookies** (Preference Cookies)

These cookies enable the website to provide enhanced functionality and personalization.

**Purpose:**
- Remembering your language preferences
- Remembering your course progress
- Storing your dashboard preferences
- Remembering your login preferences (e.g., "Remember Me")

**Examples:**
- `language_preference` - Stores your selected language (Arabic/English)
- `theme_preference` - Stores your UI theme preference (if applicable)
- `course_progress` - Tracks your progress in courses (local storage)

**Duration:** Up to 1 year

**Can be disabled?** Yes - But some features may not work properly

---

### 3. **Analytics Cookies** (Performance Cookies)

These cookies help us understand how visitors interact with our website by collecting and reporting information anonymously.

**Purpose:**
- Understanding how users navigate our website
- Identifying popular courses and content
- Improving website performance
- Measuring the effectiveness of our marketing campaigns

**Examples:**
- `_ga` - Google Analytics (if implemented)
- `_gid` - Google Analytics (if implemented)
- `_gat` - Google Analytics throttling (if implemented)

**Duration:** Up to 2 years

**Can be disabled?** Yes - Through your browser settings or our cookie consent banner

---

### 4. **Third-Party Cookies** (Marketing/Tracking Cookies)

These cookies are set by third-party services that appear on our pages.

**Purpose:**
- OAuth authentication (Google, Facebook, LinkedIn, Apple)
- Social media integration
- Payment processing (if applicable)
- Marketing and advertising (if applicable)

**Examples:**
- Google OAuth cookies (when using "Sign in with Google")
- Facebook OAuth cookies (when using "Sign in with Facebook")
- LinkedIn OAuth cookies (when using "Sign in with LinkedIn")
- Apple OAuth cookies (when using "Sign in with Apple")
- Payment gateway cookies (Stripe, PayPal, etc. - if implemented)

**Duration:** Varies by third-party provider (typically 30 days to 2 years)

**Can be disabled?** Yes - But you may not be able to use certain features like OAuth login

## Your Cookie Choices

### Browser Settings

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers allow you to:
- See what cookies you have and delete them
- Block cookies from specific websites
- Block all cookies
- Delete all cookies when you close your browser

**Note:** Blocking essential cookies may prevent you from using certain features of our website, including logging in.

### Browser-Specific Instructions

- **Chrome:** Settings → Privacy and Security → Cookies and other site data
- **Firefox:** Options → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data
- **Safari:** Preferences → Privacy → Cookies and website data
- **Edge:** Settings → Privacy, search, and services → Cookies and site permissions

### Cookie Consent Banner

When you first visit our website, you'll see a cookie consent banner where you can:
- Accept all cookies
- Reject non-essential cookies
- Customize your cookie preferences

You can change your preferences at any time by clicking the cookie settings link in the footer.
